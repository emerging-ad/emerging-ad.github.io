Why Are We Here?

Our focus: behavior.

Robust autonomy requires more than accurate perception: vehicles must learn safe, human-like decisions that generalize to the long tail of real-world driving.

Behavior Learning

How can imitation learning, offline RL, and online fine-tuning produce reliable driving behavior rather than merely reproduce expert data?

Emergence Through Self-Play

Self-play and multi-agent reinforcement learning can discover diverse skills and robust out-of-distribution behavior at scale.

Closed-Loop Worlds

Generative world models, VLA models, and GPU-accelerated simulation create controllable environments for training, evaluation, and sim-to-real transfer.
