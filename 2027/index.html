Abstract

Robust autonomous systems, spanning driving, navigation, and manipulation, require intelligent, human-like, and safe decision-making. While end-to-end models imitate expert data, self-play reinforcement learning (RL) enables diverse skill discovery and robust out-of-distribution performance without demonstrations. World models and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models drive predictive reasoning through closed-loop simulation, and GPU-accelerated multi-agent training facilitates zero-shot transfer via scale. Despite shared methodologies, cross-domain synergies and emerging behaviors remain under-explored. This workshop unites leading researchers to discuss core developments, bridge autonomous driving and robotics, and challenge fundamental assumptions on the path to robust, generalizable autonomous systems.
